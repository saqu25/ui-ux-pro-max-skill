Why Bulgaria

A direct, affordable route into European healthcare.

EU & Schengen access

Bulgaria is an EU member state; graduates gain Blue Card eligibility and easy travel across Europe.

Transparent tuition

~€10,000/year for Medicine, ~€5,000/year for Nursing — a fraction of equivalent Western European fees.

Recognised degrees

EU-recognised MD degrees and GMC/NMC/WHO-aligned nursing programs, taught fully in English.
