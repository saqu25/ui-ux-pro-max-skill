Why Bulgaria

A European Union degree, at a fraction of the cost

Bulgaria is home to some of Europe's oldest medical and technical universities, teaching in English and holding recognition from WHO, WFME and the EU. Parents choose Bulgaria for the safety of an EU nation, and students choose it for affordable tuition, modern campuses and a straightforward path to further study or practice worldwide.

  • Globally recognised degrees accepted across the EU, UK, India and beyond.
  • Affordable tuition compared to the UK, USA and Australia, with no capitation fees.
  • Safe, welcoming campuses with established Indian student communities.
  • English-medium instruction from year one, no foreign language requirement.
