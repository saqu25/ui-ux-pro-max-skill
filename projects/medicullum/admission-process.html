Admission Process

From first call to first day on campus.

Medicullum supports every stage of the process — application and document submission, preparation for the entrance exams, and the online interviews.

01

Application Submission

We map your options across Medicine and Nursing, then submit your initial application to your chosen university.

02

Document Verification

Transcripts, passport, and certificates are checked, translated and legalised where required.

03

Preparatory Course

Structured Biology & Chemistry entrance-exam preparation (≈2 months for nursing pathways).

04

Entrance Evaluation

Written exam and, for nursing pathways, an online oral interview — conducted online or on-site.

05

Final Result & Admission

Offer confirmed, followed by visa guidance, travel arrangements and arrival support for enrollment.

FAQ

Common questions

Is IELTS or another English test required?

For nursing pathways, no IELTS is required — admission is based on an entrance evaluation and online oral interview. Medicine (MD) programs are taught fully in English; specific language requirements are confirmed during your consultation.

How is the entrance exam structured?

Medicine applicants sit a written exam in Biology and Chemistry, held online or on-site depending on the session. Nursing applicants complete an entrance evaluation plus an online oral interview. Medicullum runs preparatory coaching ahead of every exam date.

What does "100% spot securing" mean?

Medicullum manages your application and document submission end-to-end, so eligible students are guided all the way to a confirmed place — with flexible budget plans available for tuition and fees.

Can I apply to more than one university?

Yes. Many students apply to two or three of our partner universities in parallel to widen their options — we'll help you plan a timeline that fits each university's deadlines and exam dates.
