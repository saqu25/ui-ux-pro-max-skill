About Medicullum

Guiding Indian Students to European Degrees, End to End

Medicullum was founded on a simple belief: studying abroad should be transparent, affordable and safe. We are the dedicated partner behind every step of your journey to Bulgaria.

Our Story

Built by counsellors who understand the anxiety of sending a child abroad

Medicullum was started by a small team of education counsellors and former international students who saw the same pattern again and again: talented students losing opportunities to poor guidance, missed deadlines and consultancies that disappeared after the admission fee was paid.

We built Medicullum to be different — a consultancy that stays involved from the first phone call to graduation day. Our partnerships with accredited Bulgarian universities are built directly with admission offices, so families always know exactly where their application stands.

Today, Medicullum supports students and parents across India pursuing Medicine, Business, Engineering, IT, Culinary Arts and Hospitality degrees in Bulgaria — with the same personal attention we started with.
