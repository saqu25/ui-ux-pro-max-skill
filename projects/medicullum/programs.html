Programs

Two tracks into European healthcare.

Whichever path fits your goals and timeline, Medicullum prepares you for the entrance exam and manages every document along the way.

Track 01

Medicine (MD)

A 6-year, English-taught Doctor of Medicine programme offered at Plovdiv, Sofia and Varna, combining pre-clinical sciences with progressively hands-on, hospital-based clinical rotations — leading to an EU-recognised medical degree.

Duration

6 years

Tuition

≈ €10,000 / year

Intake

October, annually

Entrance exam

Biology & Chemistry

Curriculum structure

  1. 1–2

    Pre-clinical sciences

    Anatomy, physiology, biochemistry and molecular biology foundations.

  2. 3–4

    Para-clinical & early clinical exposure

    Pathology, pharmacology, microbiology alongside first hospital placements.

  3. 5–6

    Clinical rotations

    Full-time rotations across internal medicine, surgery, paediatrics, obstetrics and more.

Career outcomes: hospital residency, further EU/international licensing exams (e.g. USMLE, PLAB), or postgraduate specialisation.

Track 02

Nursing (BSc)

A 4-year structured Bachelor's programme combining academic study with early and continuous clinical exposure, aligned with European education standards and approved by GMC/NMC — no IELTS required, admission is based on entrance evaluation.

Duration

4 years

Tuition

€5,000 / year

Intake

2026 cycle

Assessment

Entrance evaluation + oral interview

Why nursing in Bulgaria

  • Schengen-access country — EU Blue Card eligibility after graduation
  • No IELTS required — admission based on entrance evaluation
  • NMC & WHO-aligned programmes, consistent fee structure
  • Early and continuous clinical exposure within public health faculties
