Culinary Arts & Hospitality

Culinary Arts & Hotel Management Degrees in Bulgaria

Hands-on culinary training and internationally recognised hospitality management programs, backed by real industry placements across Bulgaria's growing tourism sector.

Why Culinary & Hospitality in Bulgaria

Practical training for a global hospitality career

Bulgaria's Black Sea coast and mountain resort towns like Bansko have built a thriving hospitality and tourism industry, giving students access to real working kitchens, hotels and resorts as part of their training.

Varna University of Management runs a dedicated Culinary Arts and Hospitality Management program with partnerships across European hotel chains, while the International Hotel Casino School in Bansko offers focused, practical diplomas in Culinary Arts and Hotel Management.

Programs combine classroom learning with paid internships, giving graduates a head start into hospitality careers across Europe, the Gulf and India's growing hospitality sector.
