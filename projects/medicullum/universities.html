Capital City · Est. 1917

Medical University of Sofia

Founded in 1917, MU-Sofia stands at the heart of Bulgarian medical education. Built within the capital's largest hospital network, this is where Bulgarian medical training took its modern form — shaped by national hospitals and over a century of clinical excellence.

Black Sea Coast · Est. 1961

Medical University of Varna

Home to one of the country's most advanced medical campuses, Varna blends modern healthcare infrastructure with an international student environment on the Black Sea coast. Clinical training is anchored at University Hospital "St. Marina," the largest hospital in northeastern Bulgaria.
