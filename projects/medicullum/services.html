What We Do

Eleven services, one dedicated team

Admission Processing

End-to-end handling of your application file, from initial submission to final offer letter.

University Applications

Preparation and submission of applications tailored to each university's specific requirements.

Document Translation

Certified translation of academic and personal documents into English or Bulgarian.

Legalisation

Apostille and embassy legalisation of documents required for university and visa submission.

Entrance Exam Preparation

Structured guidance and mock tests for university entrance exams and English proficiency.

Visa Assistance

Complete Bulgarian student visa filing support, including embassy interview preparation.

Accommodation Assistance
