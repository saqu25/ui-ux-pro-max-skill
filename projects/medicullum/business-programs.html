Business Programs

Business Administration, Finance & Management in Bulgaria

American-style liberal arts business education to EU-accredited management degrees — build an internationally respected business career at a fraction of Western tuition costs.

Why Business in Bulgaria

A globally networked business education

Bulgaria's business schools combine EU accreditation with strong international faculty, giving students exposure to real case studies, internships and exchange partnerships across Europe.

The American University in Bulgaria offers a US-accredited liberal-arts business curriculum, while institutions like UNWE and New Bulgarian University provide affordable, EU-recognised Bachelor's and Master's programs in Finance, Marketing and International Business.

Graduates are well positioned for roles in multinational companies, consulting firms and EU institutions, or to pursue further postgraduate study in the UK, Germany or the Netherlands.
