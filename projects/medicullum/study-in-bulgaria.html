Study Medicine in Bulgaria

Your Path to an MD Degree in the European Union

Six accredited medical universities, English-medium instruction and globally recognised degrees — Medicullum guides Indian students through every step of studying Medicine, Dentistry, Pharmacy, Nursing and Public Health in Bulgaria.

Overview

Why thousands of Indian students choose Bulgaria for Medicine

Bulgaria's medical universities are among Europe's oldest, with programs recognised by the WHO, WFME and the Medical Council structures across the EU. For Indian students, a Bulgarian MD offers an affordable, high-quality alternative to private medical colleges at home — without capitation fees or donation demands.

Classes are taught fully in English, campuses have established Indian student communities, and clinical training takes place in modern, EU-standard teaching hospitals from the third year onward. Degrees are recognised by India's National Medical Commission (NMC), enabling graduates to appear for FMGE/NExT.

NMC Recognised

Eligibility

What you need to apply

  • Completed 10+2 with Physics, Chemistry, Biology/Mathematics and English, scoring at least 50% aggregate (40% for reserved categories, as per NMC norms).
  • Valid NEET-UG qualifying score for the relevant academic year.
  • Minimum age of 17 years at the time of admission.
  • Valid passport with at least 18 months validity remaining.

Yes. Bulgarian medical universities are recognised by the National Medical Commission (NMC), and graduates can appear for the FMGE/NExT screening test to practice in India, subject to prevailing NMC regulations.

Yes, a valid NEET qualifying score is required for Indian students seeking an MBBS/MD equivalent degree abroad, in line with NMC guidelines.

All partner universities teach Medicine entirely in English from the first year, with no requirement to learn Bulgarian for academic purposes.

The Medicine (MD) program in Bulgaria is six years, including clinical rotations, followed by an internship as per destination-country and home-country requirements.

Airport pickup, accommodation check-in, residence permit filing and an ongoing local support contact for the duration of your degree.
